Wind Turbine Energy Prediction Based on Weather Conditions

Renewable energy, such as wind and solar energy, plays an increasing role in the global supply of electricity. With growing energy demands and environmental concerns, wind energy has become one of the most sustainable and scalable sources of power generation worldwide.

However, predicting wind energy production is challenging because it depends heavily on dynamic and fluctuating weather conditions. Wind speed, temperature, humidity, and atmospheric pressure directly impact turbine performance.

This system uses real-time weather data combined with a trained machine learning regression model to estimate wind turbine energy output. By leveraging data-driven intelligence, energy producers can improve grid stability and optimize production efficiency.
